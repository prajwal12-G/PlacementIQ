# 1. Product Overview

PlacementIQ is an AI-assisted Placement Intelligence Platform designed specifically for engineering students.

The platform helps students prepare for campus placements by analyzing resumes, evaluating ATS compatibility, matching resumes against job descriptions, conducting adaptive mock interviews, tracking placement readiness, and providing personalized improvement roadmaps.

Unlike general-purpose AI chatbots, PlacementIQ combines deterministic algorithms with AI assistance to deliver explainable, measurable, and continuously trackable placement preparation.

# 2. Vision

To become the most trusted placement preparation platform for engineering students by transforming placement preparation into a measurable, personalized, and continuously improving journey.

# 3. Problem Statement

Students currently use multiple disconnected platforms for placement preparation, including resume builders, ATS checkers, interview preparation websites, AI chatbots, and job portals.

These tools solve individual problems but fail to provide a unified view of a student's placement readiness.

As a result, students struggle to identify their weaknesses, track their improvement, and prepare systematically.

PlacementIQ solves this by integrating resume intelligence, interview preparation, progress analytics, and AI coaching into one platform.

# 4. Product Goals

Primary Goals

- Improve resume quality.
- Measure ATS compatibility.
- Match resumes against job descriptions.
- Conduct adaptive mock interviews.
- Track student progress over time.
- Generate personalized improvement plans.
- Calculate a Placement Readiness Index (PRI).

Secondary Goals

- Provide explainable recommendations.
- Help students prepare confidently for campus placements.
- Offer a modern and intuitive user experience.
