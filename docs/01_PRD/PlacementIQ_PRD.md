## 1. Project Overview

PlacementIQ is an AI-assisted Placement Intelligence Platform developed specifically for engineering students.

Unlike conventional AI chatbots or resume review tools, PlacementIQ focuses on continuously measuring and improving a student's placement readiness through structured analytics, resume intelligence, ATS evaluation, adaptive mock interviews, and personalized career roadmaps.

The platform combines deterministic algorithms with AI assistance to provide explainable, measurable, and trackable improvements throughout a student's placement journey.

## 2. Vision

To become the most reliable placement preparation platform for engineering students by helping them measure, improve, and track their placement readiness using intelligent software rather than generic AI conversations.

## 3. Problem Statement

Engineering students often prepare for placements using multiple disconnected platforms such as ChatGPT, LinkedIn, resume builders, mock interview websites, and job portals.

These platforms provide isolated services but fail to offer continuous progress tracking, measurable improvement, or personalized placement guidance.

Students frequently struggle to identify their weaknesses, monitor their progress, and understand whether they are truly placement-ready.

PlacementIQ addresses this challenge by bringing the complete placement preparation journey into one integrated platform.

## 4. Objectives

• Help students improve resumes.

• Measure ATS compatibility.

• Analyze resumes against Job Descriptions.

• Conduct adaptive mock interviews.

• Track student progress over time.

• Calculate Placement Readiness Index (PRI).

• Generate personalized improvement roadmaps.

• Provide AI-assisted career guidance.
